Projects

Simple Face Recognition System

Purpose A small Python tool that takes a photo and tells you which of a few enrolled people it contains, and answers "unknown" when the face belongs to nobody it has been enrolled on.

Stack
Python, OpenCV, NumPy.
Outcome
Recognises enrolled people from a still image on CPU; the rejection threshold was tuned on a small self-collected set so that unknown faces are declined rather than mislabelled.
What I did
  • Built the whole pipeline myself: image capture, face detection, face encoding, distance-based matching, and rendering of labelled bounding boxes.
  • Used OpenCV for capture and detection, and a compact face embedding per detected face; matching is nearest-neighbour over the enrolled embeddings with a distance threshold, so unenrolled faces are rejected instead of being forced onto the closest identity.
  • Added an enrolment step that appends each new person's embeddings to a small JSON store, so new people are added without retraining anything.
  • Kept the implementation in plain Python with NumPy and OpenCV and no training loop, so the whole thing runs on a laptop CPU.

Convolutional Neural Networks for Medical Image Classification (undergraduate thesis)

Purpose My bachelor's thesis at Taiyuan University of Technology — testing whether lightweight convolutional networks can classify medical images reliably when only a small labelled dataset is available.

Stack
Python, PyTorch.
Outcome
Both models passed 80% validation accuracy; the two-model fusion and the localisation study were the main findings reported in the thesis.
What I did
  • Implemented and compared two lightweight backbones, MobileNetV3 and ResNet-18, inside one training harness so that the comparison was like-for-like.
  • Rebuilt the MobileNetV3 classification head with a 512-unit hidden layer, Hardswish activation and Dropout, to suit a small number of classes.
  • Wrote the training and evaluation loop in PyTorch with AdamW at a 3e-4 learning rate and a StepLR schedule over 15 epochs, logging training loss, validation loss and accuracy every epoch; training ran on GPU.
  • Countered the small-sample regime with augmentation (random horizontal flip plus stronger transforms) and kept the class set identical across the training and validation splits.
  • Fused the two models at the end so that their errors would not coincide, and studied a random-localisation crop that pushes the network towards lesion regions instead of background texture.
